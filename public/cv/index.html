A Multimodal System to Support the Mental Health of Rural Woman May 2025 – August 2026
Department of Computer Science and Engineering, BRAC University (Status: Defended)
Supervisors: Dr. Amitabha Chakrabarty (Professor) & Dewan Ziaul Karim (Senior Lecturer)
  • Formulated a machine-learning framework designed to support depression and anxiety risk assessment among rural Bangladeshi women utilizing both vocal speech and transcribed text features.
  • Best Depression Model: Achieved 94.12% accuracy, 93.77% macro-F1, and 1.00 ROC-AUC using Multilingual E5 text embeddings with Linear SVM.

  • Pipeline Engineering: Evaluated diverse classification benchmarks (Linear/RBF SVM, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, KNN, Logistic Regression) and resolved clinical data class imbalances via SMOTE.
